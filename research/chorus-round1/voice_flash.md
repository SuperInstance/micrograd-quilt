**Direction: The Ripple Loom**

**The claim.** A thought is not a token sequence; it is a traveling wave that leaves a ripple wake. Quilt 2036 makes that literal. Ideas live on the quilt grid (the SuperInstance's cell lattice) as three coupled field phenomena: (1) **traveling waves** — phase fronts that sweep across cells, implementing Muller's spatiotemporal generative computation as motion over the map; (2) **ripples** — ~90Hz-local save/replay events that bind the currently-coherent distributed state into a durable granule; (3) **spirals** — phase singularities pinned at network boundaries that rotate a task's top-down/bottom-up flow and act as the "attention rotor" for the active idea. The kernel's five opcodes are the field's control surface: BIND writes a phase relationship between two cells (a wave source), LINK fixes a spiral core at a boundary, EFFECT emits a ripple (commit), VIEW samples the field at a point, TICK advances the wave equation one step. Nothing is a token; everything is a phase.

**Mechanism — ideas as field phenomena.** Each Quilt cell carries a complex amplitude and a local phase. TICK integrates a wave equation with two nonlinearities: a boundary term that nucleates spirals where phase gradients are steep (Xu/Gong's boundary condition), and a ripple gate that fires when local coherence crosses a threshold — the Verzhbinsky save event. A ripple does not store activations; it stores the *phase configuration* of the cells that co-fired, plus the spiral's winding number and rotation sense. That tuple is the granule. Consciousness, per Vishne, is then a dynamical property: a Quilt state is "conscious" of an idea when its ventral-stream-analog cells (feature maps) and prefrontal-analog cells (spiral cores) are phase-locked through a ripple train, and "unconscious" when the same content exists only as a decaying wave without a ripple commit. The JEPA-predictive GravityField proposes the next wave source; surprise is the residual between predicted phase field and measured phase field. OTOC lambda becomes the wave's Lyapunov exponent on the lattice — high lambda means the idea is chaotic and forks easily; low lambda means it is a stable attractor.

**Rewind at granule resolution.** Every ripple is a ledger entry in the FNV-chained witness log: canonical JSON of {cell phases, spiral cores, winding numbers, MOTH draw id, JEV verdict, parent granule hash}. Rewind is not checkpoint-restore; it is **re-injection**: you take any granule, re-seed the field with its phase configuration, and TICK forward. Because the wave equation is deterministic given the seed and the MOTH-certified entropy draw, replay is bit-exact and verifiable against the ledger. Forking is free — inject the same granule with a different MOTH seed and you get a sibling trajectory. Granularity is the ripple, not the token: you can rewind to the exact moment a spiral flipped rotation sense, which is the exact moment the idea changed its mind.

**JEV/MOTH/ML interlock.** MOTHQuantum supplies the certified entropy that seeds each ripple's phase jitter — this is what makes replays reproducible yet non-degenerate, and the CHSH witness is embedded in the granule so a forked run can prove it used fresh entropy. The JEV gate scores each proposed wave source before it is allowed to nucleate a spiral: canon sources (consistent with the ledger's prior phase history) pass; speculation sources are quarantined into a shadow field that can be VIEWed but not BINDed into the main lattice. ML — the JEPA predictor — sits between them: it proposes wave sources, MOTH perturbs them, JEV adjudicates, and the OTOC dial reports the ruggedness of the resulting landscape so the runtime can auto-tune lambda toward either exploration (high) or consolidation (low). The interlock is a loop: predict → perturb → judge → commit → measure ruggedness → re-tune.

**First 90 days.** Weeks 1–3: implement a 2D complex-phase lattice on the existing kernel; TICK as a leapfrog integrator; VIEW as point sampling. Weeks 4–6: ripple detector (coherence threshold + co-fire window), granule serializer, FNV ledger extension, and a replay CLI that re-injects a granule and diffs the resulting phase field. Weeks 7–9: spiral nucleation at boundaries (LINK), rotation-sense tracking, and the JEV gate wired to the granule serializer so every commit carries a verdict. Weeks 10–12: MOTH seeding of ripple jitter with CHSH witness embedded; JEPA predictor as wave-source proposer; OTOC lambda measured on the lattice and exposed as a dial. Deliverable: a runnable demo where a user plays an idea, forks it at a chosen ripple, and replays both branches to bit-exact divergence — with the ledger proving which MOTH draw and JEV verdict each branch used.

**Falsifiable prediction.** If ripples are the save/replay event, then injecting a ripple at a phase-incoherent moment (coherence below threshold) should fail to produce a stable granule — replaying it should decay within N ticks, while a ripple at coherence peak should replay stably for ≥10N ticks. Measure the survival ratio across 1000 seeded trials; the Ripple Loom predicts a sharp threshold, a feedforward transformer baseline predicts no threshold. If the threshold is absent, the direction is wrong.
